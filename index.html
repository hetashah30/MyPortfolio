About Me

I'm a Software Engineer I at MRI Software, currently working with the AI team. My journey in technology has taken me across software development, Quality Assurance, automation, and AI, giving me a well-rounded perspective on building reliable and impactful software.

I graduated with a B.E. in Information Technology from SVIT, Vasad, where I built a strong foundation in programming and software development. My technical journey has evolved from C and full-stack web development to ASP.NET Core Web API, Angular, TypeScript, and modern backend technologies.

My current focus is on AI and agentic applications. Through hands-on learning and projects, I have explored technologies including Microsoft Agent Framework, MCP, MCP Apps, LangChain, RAG, Generative AI, and Prompt Engineering. I have also gained exposure to Azure Event Hubs, AI agent and model deployment, Azure Pipelines, and GitHub Actions.

Before transitioning into software engineering, I worked as a QA Intern and QA Engineer I at MRI Software, where I gained practical experience in manual testing, API testing, regression testing, Playwright automation with C#, and Page Object Model (POM). This experience helped me develop a strong quality-focused approach to software development.

Beyond technology, I enjoy leadership, community building, and event management. As a GDG On Campus Organizer at SVIT, I organized multiple technical events, and as the Student Head of Mecia Hacks 2.0, I helped organize a large-scale hackathon.

Hackathons have also been an important part of my growth, giving me opportunities to collaborate with diverse teams, solve real-world problems, and work under tight deadlines. Outside technology, representing my school at the state level in softball taught me teamwork, discipline, perseverance, and consistency — values I continue to carry into my work.

I'm always curious, continuously learning, and excited to turn ideas into working solutions. Learn. Build. Experiment. Repeat. 🚀
